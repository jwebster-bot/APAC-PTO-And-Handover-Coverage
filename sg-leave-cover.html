Effective handover — best practice

Trigger2 working days before each person's first day off.
  • ✓Start the handover document 2 days before your PTO, plus book in a call with your primary cover and ideally secondary cover too, to ensure enough time is given to answer questions and fully explain what is needed to fully cover your desk.
  • ✓Ensure your cover is made fully aware of where every role stands — which roles need applications reviewed, and which need sourcing.
  • ✓Confirm with your cover their capacity and bandwidth; decide if a third recruiter is needed to cover certain roles or workloads.
  • ✓Send over role kick-off documents to your cover with the yellow tables and notes fully completed so it is clear.
  • ✓Make clear the likely offers and the admin that may be needed, based on how far along each role's hiring activity is.
  • ✓Intro end-stage candidates via email with the buddy and/or a third talent partner when needed.
  • ✓Ensure your PTO is clearly marked on your calendar, your Slack headline and your Gmail OOO — state the name of your cover in your Slack PTO subject line and Gmail OOO message. Copy the team into any relevant calendar events. Ensure your cover has GH access rights to the roles.
